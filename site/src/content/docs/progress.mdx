Your progress is the tick boxes you've checked at the end of each step. It's stored in this browser's local storage under keys that start with `cpdemo.v1.`, and nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data clears it too, so export it if you switch machines.

## What's stored

| Key | Value |
|---|---|
| `cpdemo.v1.<lab>.<step>` | `1` when you tick a step |
| `cpdemo.v1.track` | `full`, `core`, `customization`, or `presenter` |
| `starlight-synced-tabs__os` | Your last Windows or macOS tab choice (set by the site framework) |

Reset clears the `cpdemo.v1.` keys. It doesn't touch your repo or anything on GitHub.
